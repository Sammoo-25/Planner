- "priority_task": {"priority": "Critical"} — complete a critical priority task

- Normal daily quest: 50–100 XP

- Side quest: 30–75 XP
- Boss quest: 500–1000 XP

CONTEXT YOU RECEIVE:
- Player level and difficulty preference
- Category breakdown (which categories are under-represented)
- Completion rate of last 7 days of daily quests
